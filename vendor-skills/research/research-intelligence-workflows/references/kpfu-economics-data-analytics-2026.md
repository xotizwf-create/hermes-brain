# KFU 38.04.01 «Экономика данных и цифровая аналитика» — evidence note (2026)

## Official programme page

URL: `https://admissions.kpfu.ru/programs/38-04-01-ekonomika-profil-ekonomika-dannyh-2/`

As retrieved in July 2026, KFU states that the profile prepares specialists in:

- economics of data and digital analytics;
- intelligent information processing;
- big-data analysis;
- economic and mathematical modelling;
- digital platforms;
- forecasting;
- using analytical tools to improve business and public-administration effectiveness.

The stated programme goal is to build skills for analysing socioeconomic processes, processing/interpreting data, developing analytical models, and making managerial decisions in the digital economy.

The page shows 38.04.01 Economics, full-time, 2 years, and shared admissions information for the grouped economics profiles. Do **not** infer a profile-specific budget-seat count from this shared page.

## Curriculum evidence and confidence

The direct downloadable official curriculum PDF was not exposed in the KFU public `sveden/education` disclosure page at retrieval time. The profile appears to be new for the 2026 intake, so lack of an easily discoverable curriculum PDF should be reported as an availability limitation, not treated as proof that no plan exists.

A detailed course list is published by the secondary programme catalogue `https://kazan.postupi.online/vuz/kfu/programma-magistr/22131/`. Use it as a detailed curriculum lead, explicitly labelled as secondary, and cross-check its high-level direction against the official KFU programme page. Do not describe every listed course as independently verified by KFU until the official curriculum PDF/ОПОП is available.

## Fit analysis for AI-implementation value measurement

For an applicant who already implements AI solutions but needs to prove their business value, the strongest evidenced connections are:

1. **Econometrics + statistics + data methods**: measurement, hypothesis testing, forecasting and quantitative modelling.
2. **Investment and financial analysis**: ROI, cost-effectiveness, scenarios and economic justification.
3. **Business-analysis methodology + ERP process modelling + reengineering practicum**: tie AI impact to an observable operational process rather than a technology in isolation.
4. **Digital-economy metrics, data-driven decisions and economics of AI**: translate a technical intervention into managerial indicators and strategic choices.

Caveat: causal/counterfactual impact evaluation is a plausible research application of econometrics, but it is not explicitly named in the published high-level programme description or course list. Phrase it as the applicant's research method/goal, not as a guaranteed named course.
